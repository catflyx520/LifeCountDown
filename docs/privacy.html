Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Life Counter ("we", "the app") is a personal life-awareness tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is collected, where it lives, and what we share with third parties.

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (AsyncStorage). It is never uploaded to our servers.

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your age or date of birth
  • Your target lifespan age
  • Time Capsule messages you write to your future self
  • Notification preferences (on/off, time of day)
  • App language preference

Data sent to third parties

Google Gemini API — If you choose the "AI Estimate" mode during setup, your responses to lifestyle questions (sleep, exercise, diet, stress, etc.) and any optional medical conditions you enter are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a lifespan estimate. This data is processed by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy. This feature is optional; you may choose "Set it yourself" instead.

Firebase (Google) — The app fetches publicly available quotes and historical figure data from Firebase Firestore. No personal data is sent to Firebase.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not serve ads
  • We do not create user accounts
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not share your data with any party other than those listed above

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is transmitted to send these notifications.

Data deletion

All locally stored data can be permanently deleted at any time via Settings → Reset all data. This action is irreversible.

Children's privacy

Life Counter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at painewu@gmail.com

概述

Life Counter（"我们"、"本应用"）是一款个人生命感知工具。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明收集哪些数据、数据存储在哪里，以及我们与第三方共享的内容。

存储在您设备上的数据

以下信息使用设备本地存储（AsyncStorage）保存在您的设备上，不会上传至我们的服务器。

  • 您的姓名（可选）
  • 您的年龄或出生日期
  • 您设定的目标寿命年龄
  • 您写给未来自己的时间胶囊内容
  • 通知偏好设置（开/关、提醒时间）
  • 应用语言偏好

发送给第三方的数据

Google Gemini API — 如果您在设置过程中选择"AI 估算"模式，您对生活习惯问卷（睡眠、运动、饮食、压力等）的回答，以及您可选填写的现有病症信息，将被发送至 Google 的 Gemini API 以生成寿命估算。该数据由 Google 处理，受 Google 隐私政策约束。此功能为可选项，您可选择"手动设定"替代。

Firebase（Google） — 本应用从 Firebase Firestore 获取公开可用的名言和历史人物数据。不会向 Firebase 发送任何个人数据。

我们不收集的内容

  • 不收集分析数据或使用数据
  • 不展示广告
  • 不创建用户账号
  • 不追踪您的位置
  • 不向上述以外的任何方分享您的数据

通知

如果您开启每日提醒，通知将在您的设备本地调度。发送这些通知不会传输任何数据。

数据删除

所有本地存储的数据均可随时通过 设置 → 重置所有数据 永久删除。此操作不可撤销。

儿童隐私

Life Counter 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会主动收集儿童的信息。

政策变更

我们可能会更新本政策。顶部的"最后更新"日期反映最新修订。继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策。
